01 · Process

Process Modeling & Design

We develop physics-based and computational models to analyze, design, and optimize advanced water and chemical process systems. Fundamental transport, thermodynamics, mass and energy balances, and dynamic process behavior are translated into practical system designs.

High-recovery desalination

Dynamic models for batch, semi-batch, and hybrid reverse osmosis quantify pressure, concentration, recovery, permeate production, and energy use over time.

Membrane transport

RO, FO, and related membrane systems are modeled across changing feed concentration, pressure, temperature, polarization, scaling, and fouling conditions.

Crystallization and ZLD

Population-balance and thermodynamic models predict crystal formation, salt separation, and integrated zero-liquid-discharge performance.

Integrated system design

MATLAB and Aspen-based simulation compares process configurations and optimizes trade-offs among recovery, energy, productivity, stability, and resource use.

02 · Intelligence

AI Systems

We combine artificial intelligence and machine learning with transport theory, thermodynamics, crystallization kinetics, and dynamic models. The result is reliable prediction and decision support for behavior that mechanistic models alone cannot fully capture.

Prediction from limited data

Graph networks, tree-based ensembles, and deep learning predict molecular properties, membrane transport, and process performance from imperfect measurements.

Explainable, physical AI

SHAP attribution, sensitivity analysis, and theory-based checks test whether accurate predictions also follow physically meaningful relationships.

Hybrid models and digital twins

First-principles structures such as population balances are coupled with learned components only where theory or measurements are incomplete.

Optimization and control

Full-scale plant models and reinforcement-learning policies support adaptive operation under changing conditions, constraints, and missing sensor data.

03 · Sustainability

Sustainability & Environment

We evaluate economic feasibility, environmental performance, and energy sustainability by linking process simulation with techno-economic analysis and life-cycle assessment. This reveals trade-offs that cannot be seen through water production or energy consumption alone.

Techno-economic analysis

Capital, operating cost, energy, material demand, capacity, and productivity are evaluated to identify economically competitive process regions.

Life-cycle assessment

Environmental impacts and hotspots are quantified across electricity, chemicals, materials, waste generation, and brine management.

Water–energy integration

Renewable energy, low-grade waste heat, hydraulic recovery, and osmotic energy are integrated to reduce external energy demand.

Resource recovery

Desalination, crystallization, brine management, and ZLD strategies are assessed together to improve efficiency and reduce environmental burden.
